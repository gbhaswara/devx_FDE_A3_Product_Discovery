Delivery and VAT

All prices on this site exclude Thai VAT at 7%. VAT is charged on the order subtotal plus delivery.

Delivery is free when the order subtotal (before VAT) is ฿3,000 or more. Below that, a flat delivery fee of ฿150 applies per order.

Volume pricing is shown on each product page and applies per item, based on the number of packs of that item in the order. Sale prices replace volume pricing; the two cannot be combined.
